# TikTok review: demo video script

TikTok wants one screen recording (mp4 or mov, under 50 MB) of the whole flow, recorded on the real website
(https://autoposter.fivestars6.workers.dev) with the sandbox app. Every product and permission must appear on screen:
the TikTok login, posting with your own choices, and the draft sent to your inbox. Aim for 2–3 minutes. You can
cut the waiting parts.

## Before you record (2 minutes)

1. **Make your TikTok account private for the recording only.** In the TikTok app: Profile → ☰ → Settings and privacy →
   Privacy → Private account ON. TikTok only lets unapproved apps post directly to private accounts. Turn it back off after recording.
2. Open https://autoposter.fivestars6.workers.dev in **Chrome or Edge** (not the installed app), so the web address is
   visible. Make the window big. Log in to Autoposter.
3. In Autoposter → **Settings**, click **disconnect** next to TikTok, so the video can show the TikTok login.
4. Pick a short clip in **Library → Approved** that isn't posted to TikTok yet (under 60 s is best).

## Start recording

Press **Win + Shift + R** (Snipping Tool), select the browser window, then click **Start**.
Press **Win + Shift + R** again when done; it saves an .mp4 in Videos\Screen Recordings.

## What to show, in order

1. **The website.** Show the address bar (autoposter.fivestars6.workers.dev), then the Library.
2. **TikTok login (Login Kit, user.info.basic).** Settings → TikTok → **Connect**. TikTok's login and permission screen
   opens. Pause on it so the 3 permissions are readable, then click **Continue**. Back in Settings, TikTok shows
   **@five.stars1712**.
3. **Choosing how it's posted (video.publish).** Library → open the clip. Show the title and TikTok caption (you can
   edit a word). Scroll to **TikTok post**:
   - show your avatar and name at the top ("Posting … to this TikTok account")
   - open **Who can see this video**: it starts on "Choose…". Pick **Only me** (sandbox)
   - tick **Comment** (leave Duet/Stitch as you like)
   - turn **Disclose commercial content** ON, tick **Your brand**: the "Promotional content" label appears
   - tick **Branded content**: "Only me" becomes unavailable and the label says "Paid partnership",
     and the Branded Content Policy link appears in the consent line
   - untick both and turn disclosure OFF again, then pick **Only me** again if it was cleared
   - point at the line "By posting, you agree to TikTok's Music Usage Confirmation"
   - click **Save TikTok choices**
4. **Posting.** Click **Post now**, then **Tap again to post**. Show the message at the bottom.
   Wait (cut the wait) until the TikTok line under **Posting** says **Posted**. It can take a few minutes; the PC must be on.
5. **The result on TikTok.** Click **open** next to TikTok: the video is on your TikTok profile, visible to you only.
6. **Draft to inbox (video.upload).** Open another clip, don't set TikTok choices (and Settings → TikTok default
   choices must be empty), click **Post now** twice. When its TikTok line says
   "sent to your TikTok inbox as a draft", show that line. If the TikTok notification shows on your phone, show it too.

## After recording

1. Turn **Private account** back OFF in TikTok.
2. In the TikTok for Developers tab (Production → App review), click **Upload** under the demo video and pick the
   recording. Then tell me: I'll check the form, and you click **Submit for review**.
