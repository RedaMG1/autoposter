Privacy Policy

Last updated: 20 November 2026

Autoposter is a private tool used by a single creator (the owner of the Instagram account five.stars6) to schedule and publish short gaming clips to that creator's own Instagram, TikTok and YouTube accounts. It is not offered to the public.

What data the app uses

  • Account connection tokens issued by Instagram (Meta), TikTok and Google/YouTube when the owner connects their own accounts. They are used only to publish the owner's videos and read the connected account's name.
  • Basic profile information of the connected accounts (username, channel name, account ID) to show which account is connected.
  • Clip information: titles, captions, posting times and post status, entered by the owner.

The app does not collect data from other people, does not track visitors, and does not use cookies other than the owner's login session.

How data is stored

Data is stored in a private database on Cloudflare. Access tokens are encrypted. Video files stay on the owner's computer and are uploaded directly to the platforms at posting time.

Sharing

Data is never sold or shared. It is only sent to the platform the owner chose to post to (Instagram, TikTok or YouTube) as needed to publish a video.

Deleting data

The owner can disconnect any account in the app's Settings, which deletes its stored tokens. Access can also be removed at any time from the platform's own settings (Meta business integrations, TikTok "Manage app permissions", Google account "Third-party access"). To request deletion of all data, contact the email below.

Google user data

Autoposter's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. YouTube data is used only to upload the owner's own videos. See also the YouTube Terms of Service and Google Privacy Policy.
